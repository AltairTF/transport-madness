# Condition and maintenance belong to the Company's fleet, and breakdowns are drawn from the seeded RNG

Wear is modelled for what the player owns and decides about: Company road vehicles, trains (one value per consist), rail signals and, in the last M5 slice, road sections. Ambient cars have no Condition and do not break down by wear; they stay in the incidents that come out of the driving physics (overtaking, rear-ending, junctions). A random breakdown of an ambient car is a statistic, not a decision, and it would cost hash stability and speed in the hot loop for nothing the player can act on. It can come back with tier 1 and 2 ambient traffic.

Condition is a number from 0 to 100 that falls with the distance driven and rises with the load carried; a repair raises it. The risk of a breakdown is a hazard per metre driven, zero above a threshold and growing as Condition falls, drawn from the one simulation RNG in a fixed order (vehicles in id order, as everything else), so the same seed and commands give the same breakdowns. A breakdown is a Wreck like a crash: it blocks its lane, a rescue crew clears it, and the cargo is lost. Low Condition also lengthens braking distance, which is how it feeds rear-end collisions. Condition never lowers top speed, and there is no permanent age wear.

Maintenance is a policy the player sets at three levels, Company, Line and Vehicle, the most specific one winning ("inherit" passes the choice up). A policy says when a vehicle is due (days since its last repair, a Condition floor, either or both) and which repair it gets (Patch, Service, Overhaul). A due vehicle finishes unloading at its next stop, does not load again and goes to the nearest Depot able to do that repair, using the same garage and ghost mechanics that already let vehicles wait inside depots; the repair holds one of the depot's bays for its duration and the vehicle then returns to its Line. If no Depot can do it, the vehicle keeps running and the game warns. A policy that is off leaves the risk to the player.

Consequence: Condition, the last repair and the policy are part of the state hash from the start, so every golden hash with Company vehicles is pinned again once (and checked on macOS and Linux); worlds with no Company vehicle keep theirs. The random breakdowns and signal failures are scaled by one frequency option (off, low, normal, high) that is part of the simulation configuration and can be changed by a logged command. It does not touch the incidents that come out of the physics.

Built in M5 (`docs/project-status.md` 5.24). Two things the text above leaves to the code: only a breakdown from Condition is towed to the garage (a company vehicle wrecked in a collision is scrapped as before), and a vehicle that is serviced is not removed from the world, it goes inside its depot and comes out again, so its income, upkeep and its own policy stay with it.
